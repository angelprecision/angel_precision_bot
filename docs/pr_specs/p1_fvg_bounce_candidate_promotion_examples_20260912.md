# Canonical FVG Promotion Examples

## Promote

A proven FVG_HOLD_BOUNCE classifier result with exact canonical identity may be translated into the existing Angel Precision candidate/signal lifecycle once promotion authority is explicitly enabled.

## Do not promote

UNKNOWN, NO_SETUP, INVALIDATED, malformed identity, or unproven lower-timeframe authority produces no new FVG-generated opportunity and does not affect unrelated existing candidates.

## Isolation

Same ticker CALL vs PUT and distinct canonical FVG identities remain independent opportunities unless they are provably the same economic setup.
